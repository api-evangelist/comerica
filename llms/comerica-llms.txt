# Comerica

> Comerica Incorporated is a Texas-headquartered super-regional financial services holding company (retail, small-business, commercial, and wealth-management banking) with concentrations in Texas, California, Michigan, Arizona, and Florida. As of July 2026 Comerica publishes NO first-party developer API: developer.comerica.com is a dangling Pantheon edge (HTTP 404 "Unknown site"), api.comerica.com resets the connection, and directory trackers list no OpenAPI/Swagger specs, SDKs, or Postman collections. Consumer/small-business account data is reached indirectly through US open-finance aggregators (Plaid, MX, Finicity, Akoya) and Financial Data Exchange (FDX) APIs under the CFPB Section 1033 rule; commercial treasury/cash-management is delivered under direct customer contract (host-to-host files, portal, BAI/ISO 20022 feeds), not a public REST API. Fifth Third Bancorp completed its all-stock acquisition of Comerica in February 2026, so Comerica's future digital/API surface is being integrated into Fifth Third's platform.

## Data access (no first-party API)
- [Plaid institutions](https://plaid.com/institutions/): Aggregator path to Comerica consumer/small-business account data.
- [Akoya](https://akoya.com/): API-based data-access network for Comerica account data.
- [Financial Data Exchange (FDX)](https://financialdataexchange.org/): CFPB-recognized open-finance standard used for Section 1033 data sharing.
- [Fifth Third Bancorp](https://www.53.com/): Acquirer; Comerica's digital/API surface is being integrated here.
